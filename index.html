About this version

This is a browser-based starter app. It works on Android and desktop browsers and stores records locally on the device. It does not yet have online login, shared multi-device syncing, automated SMS, or biometric attendance. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view the records, so use a protected device and avoid storing unnecessary sensitive information.
